PART 3: SUMMARY OF COLOR TOKEN MODIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Secondary Text: Darkened from #73767a -> #525956
   - Reason: Raw sampled #73767a had 4.27:1 on cream (fails 4.5:1).
   - Result: #525956 achieves 6.72:1 on cream and 7.19:1 on white (robust PASS).

2. Muted Text: Darkened from #80888c -> #666e69
   - Reason: Raw sampled #80888c had 3.38:1 on cream and 3.61:1 on white (fails 4.5:1).
   - Result: #666e69 achieves 4.91:1 on cream and 5.25:1 on white (PASS).

3. Placeholder Text: Contrast evaluation on white vs cream:
   - Raw sampled #a0a5b1 had 2.47:1 on white and 2.31:1 on cream (fails 4.5:1).
   - Token #707773 achieves 4.59:1 on white inputs (PASS), but 4.29:1 on cream (FAILS 4.5:1).
   - Background policy: In the reference design, inputs sit inside white cards (#ffffff) where #707773 passes WCAG AA.
   - Universal token #6d7370 achieves 4.53:1 on cream and 4.84:1 on white (PASS on all surfaces).

4. Gold Accent / Text: Configured as distinct tokens per usage requirements:
   - gold = #d5ad64: decorative fills only (coins, sparkles, illustration) where contrast is not required.
   - gold-ui = #a88544: icons/large elements (3.22:1 on cream, PASS >= 3.0:1).
   - gold-text = #856529: text/links (5.05:1 on cream, PASS >= 4.5:1).

5. Caption Text: Darkened from #73767a -> #525956
   - Reason: Raw #73767a had 4.27:1 on cream.
   - Result: Reuses #525956 for 6.72:1 on cream and 7.19:1 on white.
